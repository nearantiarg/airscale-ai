Straight answers

Asked by every contractor

Will it sound like a robot?

No. It's trained on your business — your services, your prices, your tone. Warm, short, human. And you approve every script before it goes live.

What if a caller asks something it can't handle?

It escalates — transfers the call or texts you a summary instantly. You set the rules for what counts as "get me the owner."

Do I keep my phone number?

Yes. Everything runs on your existing number. Customers notice nothing except that someone always picks up.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Month to month. If it doesn't book jobs that pay for itself many times over, cancel.

How is this different from an answering service?

Answering services take messages. This books jobs — it checks your calendar, quotes windows, sends confirmations, and follows up. It's a closer, not a mailbox.

What the audit found on floridamanac.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🟡 Leaving money on the table

Two chat widgets, zero booked jobs

The site loads both Intercom and tawk.to — two competing chat popups. Neither books a job after hours; they collect messages and wait.

The agenticrx fix: One AI chat that actually resolves: triage → book → confirm, in a single conversation.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The homepage title sells loans, not cooling

The page title leads with “Residential HVAC Financing & Roof Financing” — a homeowner with no AC scrolls past loan copy to find help. Lead with the fix; offer financing at checkout.

The agenticrx fix: A rebuilt hero: “AC out? Fixed today.” — financing presented as the easy yes, not the headline.
🔴 Costing you jobs

No 24/7 or emergency language detected

For a Florida AC company, we found no stated after-hours or emergency path. When the AC dies at midnight in August, the customer's plan is unclear — so they'll call whoever answers.

The agenticrx fix: Kai is the after-hours plan: answers in seconds, books the emergency visit, texts confirmation.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

“Schedule your services” means “contact us first”

Scheduling language on the site resolves to contacting the office, not picking a confirmed time. It's a request, not a booking.

The agenticrx fix: Real booking: pick a time, get it confirmed by text, done in under a minute.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Reviews exist — buried below the fold

“Don't take our word for it” review content is on the page, but below the financing-first hero. Proof should open the conversation, not close it.

The agenticrx fix: Review stars in the hero, auto-collected after every job.

🔴 Costing you jobs

The "Schedule Today!" button leads off-site — to a Google doc

Your site's main scheduling button opens a Google Docs form on docs.google.com, not a native booking path. A homeowner with a dead AC at 11 PM leaves your site entirely to fill out someone else's form — and many never come back.

The agenticrx fix: native booking inside your site — or in chat with Ava: slot offered, name, booked. The receipt fills in live.
🔴 Costing you jobs

No after-hours path — just a phone number and an email

Your site advertises 24/7 emergency service, but past the phone number there's nothing that actually takes the job: no chat, no click-to-text, no instant booking. If the line is busy, the 2 AM caller leaves a voicemail or calls the next listing.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds, books the job, and texts the confirmation — nights, weekends, heat waves.

🟡 Leaving money on the table

Podium chat with no night shift

The Podium chat widget routes to the office — and there’s no 24/7 claim. After hours the chat is unmanned, and the customer with the dead heater in January is on their own.

The agenticrx fix: Jo answers the chat 24/7 — capturing the job and the booking while the office sleeps.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

‘Superior customer service’ — with no customer voices

The site promises “superior customer service” — yet no reviews, ratings, or testimonials appear. The boldest claim on the site has the least evidence behind it.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage and texted review requests after every job — the claim, evidenced daily.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No emergency line

No 24/7 or emergency mention was found. Monroe summers and January cold snaps don’t keep office hours — but the site assumes the customer will wait until they do.

The agenticrx fix: AI triage overnight: the emergency is captured with address and urgency — the routine question waits for morning.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No text-back

No SMS path was found. The customer who’d text but won’t call has no door — and after hours, that’s most of them.

The agenticrx fix: Missed-call text-back plus on-site text chat: every attempt to reach you becomes a captured lead.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Deals are promised — claiming one takes a call

“Great deals on air conditioning and heating products” — but there’s no online quote path for them. Every deal-seeker must dial first, and many won’t.

The agenticrx fix: Chat-based deal capture: “which deal caught your eye?” → details → booked. The promotion finally converts.

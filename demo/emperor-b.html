🔴 Costing you jobs

'We will get back to you shortly' isn't a booking

The contact form promises a callback, not a time slot. 'Shortly' at 11 PM means morning — and the customer with no heat doesn't wait for morning.

The agenticrx fix: one tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked. The receipt fills in live, and the owner gets a one-line summary.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

'24/7 SERVICE' — but the after-hours path is just the phone

The site claims round-the-clock service, yet the after-hours mechanism is the same phone line — no chat, no AI, no text-back. The claim is 24/7; the coverage isn't.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds, captures the job, and texts a confirmation — overnight, weekends, holidays.
🟡 Costing you trust

'#1 in Savannah' — with the proof below the fold

The boldest claim on the site leads the page while the customer reviews sit far down. When the claim outruns the evidence, comparison shoppers stay skeptical.

The agenticrx fix: proof next to every booking button — live review feed, star ratings beside each service.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Blog and Shop distract from the one action

Eight nav items — including Blog and Shop — compete with booking. Every extra destination is a visitor who came to schedule and left to browse.

The agenticrx fix: a rebuilt nav that leads with one action — book in 30 seconds — with content kept where it helps, not where it distracts.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No click-to-text for the mobile majority

Most homepage visits are on phones, but the only paths are dial or fill the form. A big share of customers would rather text than call — that share bounces.

The agenticrx fix: tap-to-text on every page, and Ava books the job inside the text thread.

🔴 Costing you jobs

'We'll be in touch shortly' isn't booking

The contact form's promise is 'A member of our team will be in touch shortly' — a callback queue, not a booking. No slots, no scheduling, across 11 locations.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked. The receipt fills in live, and dispatch gets a one-line summary.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

11 locations, one generic form

The form doesn't route by location — a High Point visitor joins the same queue as Orlando. Local intent, corporate queue.

The agenticrx fix: Location-aware intake: the chat knows the market and books into the right schedule.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Reviews exist — as stories, no aggregate

Customer stories are rich — video and text — but there's no star-rating summary for the skimmer who decides in four seconds.

The agenticrx fix: A live rating badge in the hero, fed by automatic review requests after every job.
🔴 Costing you trust

Corporate polish, local proof missing

The homepage sells the Southeast footprint — but the local market page shows no local reviews and no local tech faces. Scale impresses; neighbors convert.

The agenticrx fix: Local proof on every market page: local reviews, local techs, local numbers.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The membership is pitched mid-page with no instant enrollment. The recurring-revenue engine idles behind a 'learn more' link.

The agenticrx fix: Club enrollment in chat: pick a tier, add the address, done — renewals handled automatically.

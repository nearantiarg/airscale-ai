'Request Quote' is a form with no confirmation

The hero form collects name, phone, email, and service — then 'we will get back to you as soon as possible.' No slots, no timeline, no booking.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked. The receipt fills in live, and the owner gets a one-line summary.

A reviews section with no reviews in it

The homepage has a 'Here's what our satisfied customers are saying...' section — with no customer quotes, ratings, or counts visible. An empty proof section is worse than none.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed with real quotes and counts — plus review requests texted after every job.

After-hours service ends at 10 PM — then it's silent

After-hours coverage runs to 10 PM, which beats most competitors. But after 10, there's no chat, no AI, nothing — the site goes dark until morning.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds, captures the job, and texts a confirmation — even after the 10 PM cutoff.

$79 tune-up and $500-off promos with no claim path

The site has texting ('Text START to (864) 428-2638') — exactly what younger homeowners want — but it's hidden in small print instead of offered at booking.

The agenticrx fix: Texting offered up front in the booking flow — the channel customers prefer, not the one they have to hunt for.

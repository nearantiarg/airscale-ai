🔴 Costing you jobs

Paper-thin site, one way to book

The whole website is one long page — services listed in a single block, no dedicated service pages, no online booking, no instant quote. Every path ends in "call us," and callers who don't get an answer keep scrolling.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds, routes AC vs. plumbing calls to the right flow, and books the slot — 24/7.
🔴 Costing you trust

Your 4.6★ reputation is invisible on your own site

86 reviews averaging 4.6 stars on Birdeye — and the homepage shows none of them where new customers decide. When your reviews are off-site, your competitor's on-site reviews win.

The agenticrx fix: a live review feed on your homepage, auto-replies to every review, and review requests texted after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

AC and plumbing share one "call us"

You're really two businesses — air conditioning and plumbing, plus a DIY store — but the site funnels both through one generic phone CTA. A water-heater emergency and a dead AC deserve different questions and different bookings.

The agenticrx fix: trade-specific booking flows — "water heater" gets its own questions, its own estimate, its own slot.

Closed Sundays — Florida AC emergencies aren't

Your listing shows Mon–Sat 24 hours, closed Sunday. One day a week of "leave a message" — in Orlando, in the summer, for a company whose name is air conditioning.

The agenticrx fix: Sundays become answered, booked, and texted-back — with the owner getting a one-line Monday-morning summary, not a voicemail pile.

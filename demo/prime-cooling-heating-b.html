🔴 Costing you jobs

24/7 emergency with no one to talk to

The site promises 24/7 emergency services — but no chat or AI exists, and the only written path is a form built for business hours. At 2 AM the promise is a phone line and a hope.

The agenticrx fix: Otto answers in 8 seconds, triages the emergency, and confirms help is coming — the 24/7 claim finally has coverage.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The free-quote form is 10 fields

“Get a Free Quote” starts with a 10-field WPForms gauntlet. Every extra field is a reason to abandon it — especially on a phone, especially with no AC.

The agenticrx fix: Chat-based quote capture: address, problem, preferred time — three answers, not ten boxes.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No phone number in the readable page text

No tap-to-call number was found in the site’s readable content. The fastest conversion path a panicked customer has is missing where they’d look for it.

The agenticrx fix: A persistent tap-to-call button plus missed-call text-back: every call attempt becomes a captured lead.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No reviews on the site

No ratings, testimonials, or review badges were found. The emergency customer is asked to trust a stranger at the worst possible moment — with no evidence.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage and texted review requests after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No online booking

There’s no scheduling flow — no slot picker, nothing completable in 30 seconds. The customer who’d book without calling bounces.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat → slot offered → booked, with a confirmation text.

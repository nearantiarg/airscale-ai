The 24/7 emergency line is still just a phone line

Scheduled hours are Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM; after that, the “24/7 Emergency Service” is one number: (786) 306-2025. No chat, no AI, no text-back. If the line is busy at 1 AM, the restaurant with the warming walk-in calls the next company.

The agenticrx fix: Luna answers in 8 seconds, triages residential vs. commercial refrigeration, and dispatches — the 24/7 promise finally has someone behind it.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

‘What Our Happy Customers Are Saying’ says nothing

The testimonial section header exists — but no customer quotes were visible beneath it. A visitor who scrolls to the proof and finds none trusts the site less than if the section didn’t exist.

The agenticrx fix: Real reviews pulled onto the homepage automatically, plus texted review requests after every job so the section fills itself.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The stat counters read zero

The homepage counters show “0+” years of service, “0+” happy clients, “0/7” emergency availability — broken counters sitting under confident copy. Every visitor who notices them doubts everything above them.

The agenticrx fix: A rebuilt page where the numbers are real and verifiable — or honest copy that doesn’t need counters.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Commercial clients can’t triage after hours

Walk-in freezers, coolers, and restaurant equipment are a headline specialty — but at 1 AM a restaurant manager gets the same phone line as everyone else, with no way to flag “thousands in inventory at risk.”

The agenticrx fix: AI triage that captures business vs. home, equipment type, and urgency — the on-call tech sees the inventory-at-risk calls first.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Book Now buttons with no conversational path

“Book Now” and “Get A Quote” buttons are everywhere — but there’s no chat, text, or instant engagement behind them. The customer taps, fills, and waits.

The agenticrx fix: Chat-based booking with instant confirmation text — the tap becomes a scheduled job in 30 seconds.

What the audit found on callzenair.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

Every call-to-action is “call us”

The homepage's dominant action is "Call Now", repeated across sections — the whole funnel collapses to one phone number. A big share of homeowners would rather book online than dial, and that share bounces.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds, captures the job, and texts a confirmation — overnight, weekends, holidays.
🔴 Costing you jobs

“Book Now” opens a contact form

The "Book Now" button leads to a "Let's Connect" form — no calendar, no time slots, no confirmation. The visitor types into a void and waits for a callback that may never come.

The agenticrx fix: one tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked. The receipt fills in live, and the owner gets a one-line summary.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No hours published anywhere

The homepage lists no office hours at all — a customer can't tell whether anyone will pick up at 7 AM, 7 PM, or on a Sunday. Uncertainty kills the call.

The agenticrx fix: published hours with an AI that answers and books around the clock — so the hours question never costs a job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

SEO copy pushes the action down

The homepage repeats near-identical service-plus-city phrasing section after section ("full-service furnace services in Duluth, GA", "complete AC services in Duluth, GA") — density that buries the booking action.

The agenticrx fix: a rebuilt page that leads with one action — book in 30 seconds — with the SEO kept where Google needs it, not where humans trip on it.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Zero social proof on the page

A company with years of local history shows no reviews, no ratings, no testimonials on its homepage. First-time visitors get claims, not proof.

The agenticrx fix: a live review feed on the homepage, auto-replies to every review, and review requests texted after every job.

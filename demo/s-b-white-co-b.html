What the audit found on sbwhiteco.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

No contact form — anywhere

“Get in Touch” is an address, a phone number, and an email. A facility manager who wants a bid on a chiller retrofit can't start the conversation online — they have to call cold.

The agenticrx fix: a project-inquiry flow in chat — facility type, scope, timeline — delivered to the estimator as a qualified lead.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No bid-request path for commercial leads

Every commercial inquiry must arrive by phone or cold email. There's no “request a bid,” no project form, no qualification step — the highest-value leads get the least structure.

The agenticrx fix: structured bid requests that pre-qualify the project — so the estimator calls back with context, not questions.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No rapid-response story for downtime clients

The copy understands that plant shutdowns equal lost revenue — but offers no emergency line, no rapid-response promise, no after-hours path for the client losing money by the hour.

The agenticrx fix: a published rapid-response path with AI triage — the downtime call becomes a dispatched crew in minutes.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

“Real examples of our work” — without examples

The page invites visitors to “explore some real examples of our work below” — but the industry sections are generic descriptions, no named projects, no photos with context, no case studies.

The agenticrx fix: named project stories with photos and outcomes — the portfolio that wins the next hospital or school bid.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No hours published

The site lists no business hours — a small thing that makes a 130-year-old company look less established than it is.

The agenticrx fix: published hours with 24/7 AI coverage behind them — every inquiry answered, every time.

🔴 Costing you jobs

Two different phone numbers on one site

The header shows (770) 872-4168, but the site's own accessibility notice lists (678) 470-8324. A homeowner in a hurry dials whichever they see first — and half of them reach the wrong line.

The agenticrx fix: One number everywhere. The AI answers it around the clock, so 'which number' stops mattering.
🔴 Costing you jobs

No office hours posted anywhere

The site claims 24/7 live answering but never shows actual office hours. Visitors can't tell whether they're calling a staffed office or a call center — and many won't call at all.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds with your real availability, so the hours question answers itself.
🔴 Costing you trust

The first thing visitors see is a legal notice

The homepage leads with an ADA accessibility notice before any service content. Your first impression is legal text, not comfort, not credentials.

The agenticrx fix: A rebuilt page that leads with one action — book in 30 seconds — and keeps compliance copy where it belongs.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

A mega-menu with 100+ links swallows the booking path

Cooling, heating, plumbing, electrical — each exploded into dozens of sub-pages. The 'book' action competes with over a hundred navigation links on every page.

The agenticrx fix: One chat box that asks 'what's wrong?' and routes to the right trade automatically.

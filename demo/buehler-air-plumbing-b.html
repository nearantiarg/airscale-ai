The homepage's only conversion path is "call Buehler Air Conditioning & Plumbing today". No online booking, no quote form, no click-to-text on the page. A big share of homeowners would rather book online than dial — that share bounces.

The agenticrx fix: one tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked. The receipt fills in live, and the owner gets a one-line summary.

The copy claims a five-star Google rating, plus BBB A+ accreditation and the Carrier President's Award — but the homepage shows not a single customer review. When your proof lives elsewhere, competitors with on-site reviews look more credible.

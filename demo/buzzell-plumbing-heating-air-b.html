🔴 Costing you jobs

The chat widget is a message-taker, not a problem-solver

The site runs a Podium chat widget — but after hours it's an away message and a form. Nobody triages the burst pipe, nobody books the visit, nobody quotes the water heater. The conversation starts and then… waits for morning.

The agenticrx fix: Leo handles the whole chat: triage, booking, and confirmation — at 2 AM exactly like 2 PM.
🔴 Costing you jobs

No online booking path observed

Residential, commercial, maintenance agreements — every service line funnels to call or contact form. There's no “pick a time” anywhere on the site we audited.

The agenticrx fix: Chat-based booking with live slot offers and instant confirmation text.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

“24/7 emergency” meets a closed office

Emergency service is advertised, but the after-hours experience is the widget's away message. A 2 AM caller with no heat has to hope someone calls back.

The agenticrx fix: The AI is the after-hours team: answers, qualifies, and dispatches per your rules.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No customer reviews on the homepage

For a company this established, the homepage shows no ratings, no testimonials, no review count — the trust story is told nowhere the visitor looks first.

The agenticrx fix: Live review feed on the homepage, auto-replies, and post-job review requests by text.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

A huge service menu with no guided path

Residential, commercial, new construction, water quality — the nav is a wall of links. A stressed homeowner with one problem gets no “start here” triage.

The agenticrx fix: “What's going on?” — three questions, then the right service, the right tech, the right slot.

What the audit found on betteracrepair.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

Every conversion path is "give us a call"

The site's only call to action is a phone call for a "no pressure estimate" — no online booking, no instant quote, no callback in seconds. A homeowner with a dead AC at 10 PM can leave a message — or call someone who answers.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds, triages the issue, and books the slot — nights, weekends, heatwaves. The owner gets a one-line summary.
🔴 Costing you trust

An 888 number, not a Fort Myers one

The headline number on the site is a toll-free (888) number — no local area code anywhere. Fort Myers homeowners pick the shop that feels local; an 888 number makes you feel like a call center.

The agenticrx fix: a local number on every page — with AI answering every call so "local" never goes to voicemail.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

One page lists North Fort Myers, Pflugerville TX, and Marietta GA

The service-area copy names three states on one page. A North Fort Myers visitor can't tell if you're their neighborhood shop or a national template — and "near me" searches reward the shop that looks local.

The agenticrx fix: one clear service-area story per page — "Fort Myers first" — with the call handled locally even when the team is on a job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No hours, no address, anywhere

The site lists neither hours nor a street address. Customers can't tell when you're open, where you're based, or whether to expect an answer tonight — so they guess, and guessers call the next listing.

The agenticrx fix: clear hours plus AI that answers 24/7 — the "open" question stops mattering, and every after-hours caller still becomes a job.

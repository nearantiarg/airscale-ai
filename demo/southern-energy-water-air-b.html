What the audit found on southernewa.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

“Call Us” is the only path

The homepage's conversion path is a “Call Us” link — no online booking, no scheduling, no quote form. Every digital visitor must pick up the phone or leave.

The agenticrx fix: one tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked. The owner gets a one-line summary, and the customer gets a confirmation text instantly.
🔴 Costing you trust

No customer proof on the page

The 2018 BBB Torch Award for Marketplace Ethics is displayed — but there are no customer reviews, no testimonials, no ratings anywhere on the homepage. Awards without customer voices feel institutional, not trusted.

The agenticrx fix: a live review feed on the homepage, auto-replies to every review, and review requests texted after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Four trust sections, one copied paragraph

“Highest Standards,” “Trusted Service Vendor,” “Property Management Professionals,” and “Building Technology” all repeat the same one-year-warranty paragraph word-for-word. Copy-paste filler where differentiation should be.

The agenticrx fix: four sections, four real proof points — reviews, job counts, guarantees, credentials. Every pixel earns the booking.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

24/7 emergency, one ringing phone

24/7 emergency plumbing is promised — but the after-hours path is a single phone number with no text or chat fallback. A burst pipe at 2 AM needs a system, not a hope.

The agenticrx fix: an AI that answers every after-hours contact, triages the emergency, and dispatches — so 24/7 is real.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No hours published anywhere

The homepage lists no business hours — customers can't tell when anyone is available, and there's nothing setting expectations after hours.

The agenticrx fix: published hours backed by 24/7 AI coverage — the “are you open?” question never costs a job.

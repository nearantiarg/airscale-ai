🔴 Costing you jobs

No price anywhere

'Simple monthly rental' — with no number. Every builder has to call to learn the rate, and half of them won't bother.

The agenticrx fix: Transparent starting rates plus an instant quote flow: square footage in, estimate out, call booked.
🔴 Costing you jobs

'Two Calls. That's It.' — both are phone calls

The entire funnel is call Joe or email. No quote form, no availability check, no way to start at 10 PM from the trailer.

The agenticrx fix: A chat that quotes the job — location, size, timeline — and books the site walk in one thread.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

5.0 on Google, buried mid-page

Verified builder reviews exist — but they sit far below the fold with no badge up top. The best proof on the site is the hardest to find.

The agenticrx fix: The 5.0 badge in the hero, review quotes beside the quote form, and automatic review requests after every rental.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No service-area checker

Charleston and Tampa Bay are listed as text. A builder in Savannah — or Sarasota — has to call to ask if they're covered.

The agenticrx fix: A coverage checker in chat: enter the job-site ZIP, get an instant yes and a quote.
🔴 Costing you trust

Founder-forward, company-thin

The site leans on founder Joe Lloyd's story; beyond two named people there are no scale signals — fleet size, units in the field, active sites.

The agenticrx fix: Proof points on the page: units deployed, builders served, and the reviews to back them.

The agenticrx fix: Chat booking in 30 seconds — slot offered, name captured, confirmation texted. The phone keeps ringing for those who prefer it.
🔴 Costing you jobs

No after-hours answer stated anywhere

Nothing on the site says who picks up at 11 PM — no 24/7 line, no after-hours message, no AI. A Jacksonville homeowner with a dead AC overnight has to hope someone answers.

The agenticrx fix: Max answers in 8 seconds at any hour, captures the job, and the owner gets a one-line summary in the morning.

The free service call for second opinions (with a competitor's invoice) is a strong offer — but claiming it starts with dialing. No online claim path exists.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat claims the offer and books the visit — the invoice photo can be texted in.

The Trustindex widget shows EXCELLENT — based on 50 reviews, but there's no automated review request after jobs. Growth of that count depends on customers remembering on their own.

The agenticrx fix: Automatic review requests texted after every job, with auto-replies to each review.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The site never states when the office is staffed, so customers can't tell whether a call at 7 PM reaches a person or voicemail.

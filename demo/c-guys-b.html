What the audit found on theacguysinc.net — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

Emergency service is members-only

The site states emergency service is for maintenance members only. A non-member with a dead AC at midnight learns they’re not covered — and there’s no path to become covered at midnight.

The agenticrx fix: Gus signs the caller up for the maintenance plan on the spot — the emergency gets covered and the plan gets sold, at 2 AM.
🔴 Costing you jobs

Podium chat sleeps after 5 PM

Office hours are Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM — “this includes weekends, holidays, and any time outside” those hours. The Podium chat widget has nobody behind it on nights and weekends.

The agenticrx fix: AI answers the chat 24/7 — capturing the job and the plan signup while the office sleeps.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No online booking

Every path to a job is “call us” or the chat widget. There’s no scheduling flow a customer can complete on their own in 30 seconds.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat → slot offered → booked, with a confirmation text. The phone stays for the callers.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

NATE-certified techs, no customer voices

Certification is claimed on the site, yet no reviews or testimonials appear in the readable content. Credentials persuade technicians; reviews persuade homeowners.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage and texted review requests after every job — proof that speaks homeowner.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The quote path is a phone call

No quote form was detectable on the site. The homeowner who wants a number before a conversation has no way to start one.

The agenticrx fix: Chat-based estimate capture — address, system age, preferred time — so the tech arrives with context and the job is half-sold.

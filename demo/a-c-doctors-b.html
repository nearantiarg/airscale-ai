'Book your appointment online' leads to a form, not a booking

The process section promises online booking, but the path ends at a contact form with no slot picker and no confirmation. The appointment isn't real until someone calls back.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked. The receipt fills in live, and the owner gets a one-line summary.
🔴 Costing you trust

A reviews section with no reviews in it

The homepage has a 'What Our Customers Say' section with no customer quotes, no rating, and no review count visible. An empty proof section is worse than none at all.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed with real quotes and counts — plus review requests texted after every job.

The site’s conversion path is “call or fill out the contact form.” No scheduler, no click-to-text, no chat. Homeowners who’d rather book online than dial simply bounce.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked. The receipt fills in live, and the owner gets a one-line summary.

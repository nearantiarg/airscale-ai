🔴 Costing you jobs

Two different phone numbers

The site’s own meta description tells customers to call 470-940-4802 — while the published business number is (678) 653-0658. A homeowner who saves the wrong one reaches a stranger, or nobody.

The agenticrx fix: One number everywhere, tracked to the source. Every call, text, and chat lands in one inbox — nothing rings into the void.
🔴 Costing you jobs

No way to book without calling

No online booking, no quote form, and no chat were detectable on the homepage. After the office closes, the site is a billboard — it can’t take a job.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked. The owner gets a one-line summary; the customer gets a confirmation text.
🔴 Costing you jobs

Nothing answers after hours

No hours, no 24/7 claim, and no chat or AI appear anywhere in the readable site. A homeowner with no AC at 11 PM has exactly one option: wait until morning, or call a competitor.

The agenticrx fix: Milo answers in 8 seconds, captures the job, and texts a confirmation — overnight, weekends, holidays.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Zero social proof on the homepage

No reviews, ratings, or testimonials appear in the site’s readable content. At the moment of decision, the customer sees claims with no evidence behind them.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage, auto-replies to every review, and review requests texted after every job.

Only about 220 characters of readable text were found in the static page — the content renders in JavaScript. Customers searching “air conditioning repair Smyrna GA” may never find the page at all.

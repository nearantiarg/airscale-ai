What the audit found on pioneercomfort.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🟡 Leaving money on the table

No live greeter for a 24/7 multi-trade shop

The site claims 24/7 and offers online booking — but no chat or AI exists. The customer choosing between “no AC,” “no hot water,” and “sparking outlet” gets no guidance before committing to a form.

The agenticrx fix: Pia greets every visitor, asks “what’s going on?”, and routes by trade and urgency — the right tech gets the right job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The form is 11 fields

First name, last name, email, phone, message, and more — 11 fields before the customer has said what’s wrong. On mobile, that’s a wall.

The agenticrx fix: Conversational intake: one question at a time, done in under a minute — on any phone.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Four trades, one front door

Heating, AC, plumbing, and electrical all funnel through the same form and phone line. There’s no triage — “no hot water” and “sparking outlet” wait in the same queue.

The agenticrx fix: AI triage by trade and urgency: the electrical emergency jumps the queue with full context attached.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

‘Highly rated’ with no ratings shown

The meta description calls Pioneer highly rated — yet no stars, scores, or customer quotes appear on the homepage. The claim hangs in the air with nothing holding it up.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed with real quotes on the homepage, plus texted review requests after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No text-back option

No SMS path was found. The customer who’d text a photo of the leaking water heater — instead of describing it on a call — has no door.

The agenticrx fix: Text-back and on-site text chat with photo-friendly intake: the customer shows, not tells.

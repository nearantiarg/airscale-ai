The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds, triages the emergency, and books the visit — overnight, weekends, holidays.

“Send a Request” is a waiting room

The online path is "send a request online and we'll follow up" — a plain form with no calendar, no time slots, no confirmation. The visitor submits and waits; urgency leaks out of the funnel.

The agenticrx fix: chat-based booking — slot offered, name captured, confirmation texted. No waiting room.

Free estimates have no self-serve path

You promise free estimates on replacements and installations — but claiming one means calling or waiting on a form follow-up. The highest-intent action on the site has the most friction.

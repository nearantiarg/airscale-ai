The agenticrx fix: A working booking flow: pick a day and time in chat, get a confirmation text. The promise finally keeps itself.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No live answer after hours

No chat, no AI answering. After hours, visitors get the phone number or nothing — while the guarantees promise responsiveness.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds, captures the job, and texts a confirmation — overnight, weekends, holidays.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Reviews depend on a third-party widget

Social proof loads from an external review widget. If that script stalls, the page shows no proof at all — and the business doesn’t control the experience.

The agenticrx fix: A native review feed on the homepage — fast, on-brand, and always under the company’s control.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Five guarantees, no digital way to claim one

The written guarantees are a real differentiator — but there’s no online path to invoke any of them. They’re marketing, not mechanics.

The agenticrx fix: Guarantee claims handled in chat — logged, timestamped, and routed to the owner instantly.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No hours posted

Nothing on the site says when the office is staffed. Visitors can’t tell whether anyone will pick up right now.

The agenticrx fix: Posted hours plus AI coverage outside them — the site always answers, and says when a human follows up.

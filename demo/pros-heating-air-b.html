‘Don’t wait to call’ — but calling is the only option

“Emergency? Don’t wait to call PROS” — yet there’s no chat, no text, no booking. If the line is busy, the customer waits anyway — exactly what the headline told them not to do.

The agenticrx fix: Lou answers in 8 seconds, captures the emergency with address and details, and confirms help is coming — no waiting, no busy signal.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The contact form is 16 fields

The Gravity form runs to 16 fields. The emergency customer — the one the headline is shouting at — is asked for everything before anything.

The agenticrx fix: Conversational intake: problem, address, time — three answers, not sixteen boxes.

4.9 stars in the markup, zero reviews on the page

The page markup claims 4.9/5 — yet no reviews, quotes, or badges appear in the readable content. The score exists for Google; the customer sees none of it.

The agenticrx fix: Real review quotes pulled onto the homepage automatically — the markup claim becomes visible proof.

No after-hours answer besides the phone

No 24/7 claim and no chat were found. The emergency framing sets an expectation — “don’t wait” — that the site can’t fulfill after hours.

The agenticrx fix: AI coverage overnight and weekends: the “don’t wait” promise holds at 2 AM, not just 2 PM.

No online booking

There’s no scheduling flow — nothing a customer can complete in 30 seconds on a phone. The non-emergency customer has the same one door as the emergency one: call.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat → slot offered → booked, with a confirmation text.

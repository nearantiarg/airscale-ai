🔴 Costing you jobs

Office hours end at 4:00 PM. Emergencies don't.

The site posts Mon–Fri, 8:00 AM–4:00 PM office hours with a technician "on call 24/7" for emergencies — but after 4 PM the only path is "call us," with no chat, no text-back, and no AI answering. The emergency promise runs on one phone line.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds, qualifies the emergency, and dispatches the on-call tech — nights, weekends, holidays.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

"Schedule Service" is call-or-form

There's no online booking, no live slots, and no instant confirmation for the after-hours caller. A form submitted at 9 PM waits for the office to open at 8 AM.

The agenticrx fix: real slots offered in chat, booked in under a minute, text confirmation before they close the tab.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Free quotes behind a form

Your two best hooks — free HVAC replacement quotes and free second opinions — both require submitting a form and waiting. The fastest way to win a shopper is to answer before the competitor does.

The agenticrx fix: quote requests handled live in chat — Ava captures the details and books the visit on the spot.

450+ five-star reviews, no badge to prove it

The claim sits mid-page, and recent Google reviews are embedded individually with owner replies — but no aggregate star badge greets the visitor at the top, where the decision gets made.

The agenticrx fix: a live 5-star badge in the hero with fresh quotes rotating below — the reputation sells before the scroll.

No SMS anywhere on the site

Contact is phone and dispatch@extremetempac.com only — no text line. Homeowners who'd rather text than call have no path to you at all.

The agenticrx fix: a text line with Ava on it — every message answered in seconds, every conversation ending in a booked job.

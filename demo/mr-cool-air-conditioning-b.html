What the audit found on mrcoolairconditioning.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

The quote form has 25 fields

The Gravity quote form runs to 25 fields. On a phone, in July, with no AC, nobody finishes that — they abandon it and call whoever’s easiest to reach.

The agenticrx fix: Conversational intake: one question at a time — problem, address, time — completed in under a minute on a phone.
🔴 Costing you jobs

No tap-to-call number in the page text

No phone number was found in the site’s readable text. The fastest path a panicked customer has — tap, talk, booked — is missing at the moment of panic.

The agenticrx fix: A persistent tap-to-call button plus missed-call text-back: every call attempt becomes a captured lead.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Emergency is mentioned — an emergency path isn’t

Emergency repair language appears on the site, but there’s no 24/7 claim, no after-hours number, and no chat. The “emergency” customer gets the same 25-field form as everyone else.

The agenticrx fix: AI triage that spots the emergency, captures the details, and confirms help is coming — instantly, at any hour.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No reviews or ratings anywhere

The site promises “honest, affordable” service — but no customer reviews, ratings, or testimonials appear. Honesty claimed without proof is just a slogan.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage and texted review requests after every job — proof that backs the promise.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No chat, no text, no booking

The 25-field form is the only digital door. No live chat, no SMS path, no scheduling — three entire categories of customer have nowhere to go.

The agenticrx fix: On-site AI chat, text-back, and one-tap booking: every kind of customer gets a door that actually opens.

🟡 Leaving money on the table

'Request Service' is a form, not a booking — even at 24/7

The site is open 24 hours, 7 days — rare and valuable. But the digital path is still a form: name, phone, comments, submit. The 24/7 advantage isn't bookable.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked at 2 AM. The 24/7 claim becomes a 24/7 booking engine.
🔴 Costing you jobs

'Connect with us online or give us a call' — vague when it matters

The booking copy tells visitors to 'connect with us online' without saying what happens next. Vague CTAs leak the shoppers who need certainty.

The agenticrx fix: The AI sets the expectation in the first reply: 'A tech can be there tomorrow between 9–11. Want me to hold it?'
🔴 Costing you trust

73 reviews published — the count should lead the hero

The site publishes 73 reviews with named quotes across Google, Facebook, and its own platform. Excellent proof — but the hero doesn't show the count.

The agenticrx fix: The review count and rating move above the fold, updating live — the proof leads instead of following.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No chat for the 'quick question' shopper

Commercial refrigeration, sheet metal, duct sealing — considered services shoppers research. But every question routes to a call or a form.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers the scope and pricing questions in chat and hands off a warm, qualified lead.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The review widgets are third-party embeds, not owned proof

Reviews render through third-party review widgets. If the widget breaks or the contract lapses, the proof vanishes with it.

The agenticrx fix: Reviews owned on your own site and syndicated outward — the proof survives any vendor change.

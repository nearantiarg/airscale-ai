🔴 Costing you trust

'Open 24 hrs' on the page, 'Mon–Fri 7:30–5' in the hours markup

The page claims open 24 hours, Mon–Sun — but the structured hours say Mon–Fri 7:30am–5pm. Google shows the markup, not the headline. Contradictions kill trust.

The agenticrx fix: One true schedule everywhere — site, markup, and listings — with AI covering every hour you're actually closed.
🔴 Costing you jobs

The directory phone doesn't match the site phone

Listings show (910) 877-8113; the site pushes (910) 210-0399 (plus an alt (910) 399-8157). Three numbers in the wild.

The agenticrx fix: One number everywhere, answered by AI around the clock — so 'which number' stops mattering.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

'Get an online estimate' leads to a form, not an estimate

The estimate CTA opens a contact form — first name, last name, message, submit. No estimate, no timeline, no confirmation. The promise and the path don't match.

The agenticrx fix: The AI gives a real estimate range in chat and books the visit — the promise kept in one conversation.
🔴 Costing you trust

BBB A+ claimed with no badge to click

The site claims BBB accreditation and an A+ rating — but there's no badge and no link. Trust claims without verification read as decoration.

The agenticrx fix: Trust badges displayed and linked — the claim verifies in one click.

The form's fine print bundles autodialer text-message consent into the submit. Consent walls shrink conversion — and annoy the exact shoppers you want.

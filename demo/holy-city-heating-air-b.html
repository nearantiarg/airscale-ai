What the audit found on holycityhvac.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🟡 Leaving money on the table

Three booking promises, one contact form

“Book Same-Day Service,” “Schedule Service,” and “Instant Estimate” all link to /contact — the same form. Three different promises, one identical destination.

The agenticrx fix: one tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked. The receipt fills in live, and the owner gets a one-line summary.
🔴 Costing you jobs

“Call Until 9 PM” — then it's the form

The hero invites calls until 9 PM — admirably late. But after 9, the site's best answer is the same contact form. No chat, no AI, no text-back.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds, captures the job, and texts a confirmation — overnight, weekends, holidays.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The “Shop” exists — booking still funnels to the form

There's an e-commerce-style Shop section, but service booking still ends at /contact. The most transactional part of the site is the least transactional.

The agenticrx fix: chat-based booking with live slots — the shop sells parts, the chat books the visit.
🔴 Costing you trust

No live review feed on the homepage

A premium brand — Daikin Comfort Pro, wrapped fleet, family story — with no live review proof on the homepage. The brand earns stars; the site should show them.

The agenticrx fix: a live review feed on the homepage, auto-replies to every review, and review requests texted after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

“Instant Estimate” isn't instant

The button says instant. The destination is a form someone reads later. Nothing about it is instant — and visitors can tell.

The agenticrx fix: chat-based estimate capture — a few quick questions, instant ballpark, details only if they want the visit.

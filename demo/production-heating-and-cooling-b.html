What the audit found on productionair.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🟡 Leaving money on the table

The booking button opens an empty box

“Schedule an Appointment” opens a pop-up modal whose booking area is an empty container, filled only if a third-party script loads. If the script fails, the button does nothing.

The agenticrx fix: Native booking in chat — no pop-up, no third-party script, no silent failure.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No live answer after hours

No chat, no AI. “Reliable 24/7 service” is claimed in the header, but after hours the digital path is the same modal or the phone line.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds, captures the job, and texts a confirmation — overnight, weekends, holidays.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Testimonials with no ratings or dates

Long customer letters appear with first names only — no stars, no dates, no source. Heartfelt, but unverifiable.

The agenticrx fix: Real reviews pulled live from the Google profile — names, stars, and dates included.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No aggregate rating anywhere

No stars, no review count, no badges — despite 70 years in business and strong claims about honesty and trust.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage, auto-replies to every review, and review requests texted after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

“Free estimates” — via the same modal

Complimentary estimates are offered, but claiming one means the estimate-request pop-up — another form-and-wait, not a booking.

The agenticrx fix: Chat-based estimate capture — address, system age, preferred time — confirmed instantly.

What the audit found on mjservicesinc.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🟡 Leaving money on the table

"Book Online" leads to a form page, not a calendar

The "Book Online" button goes to a /book page — a form, not live scheduling. Visitors expecting to pick a time get fields instead.

The agenticrx fix: Real booking: one tap in chat → live slot offered → name → booked, with confirmation texted instantly.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Recent Google reviews shown — but no aggregate badge

The homepage shows named recent Google reviews (e.g. Lisa Godwin, September 2026) — genuine proof — but no overall rating or count badge. The evidence is there; the summary isn't.

The agenticrx fix: A live aggregate badge next to the reviews, plus automatic review requests after every job to keep it growing.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Office hours end at 4 PM; after-hours is emergency-only

Posted hours are Mon–Sat 8 AM–4 PM, with emergency/after-hours coverage to 4 AM. Overnight, everything funnels through the phone — no triage, no booking.

The agenticrx fix: Ava triages overnight calls — true emergencies dispatched, routine jobs booked for morning — with a confirmation texted instantly.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No chat or texting path

Phone and form are the only doors in. The visitor who'd rather text than call has no way in after hours.

The agenticrx fix: Every page gets a text-us button wired to the same AI that answers the phones — one inbox for every conversation.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Financing competes with booking on the same screen

Financing CTAs share prime screen space with booking — two competing actions at the moment of decision, splitting the click.

The agenticrx fix: One primary action — book — with financing offered inside the flow after the slot is chosen.

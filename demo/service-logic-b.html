The agenticrx fix: Every location page with its own click-to-call, chat, and dispatch triage — the national brand, locally reachable.
🔴 Costing you jobs

Built to acquire, not to answer

‘Join Service Logic’ — the acquisition pitch — sits beside the service CTAs. The site speaks to sellers as loudly as to customers. The buyer with an emergency is an afterthought.

The agenticrx fix: Separate the funnels: customers get dispatch in one tap; sellers get the partnership story. Never the same page.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

One account manager, zero instant paths

The model is ‘one account manager’ — no chat, no AI, no emergency dispatch path for the 2 AM rooftop failure. Enterprise buyers expect a ticket, not a voicemail.

The agenticrx fix: AI triage that opens the ticket, pages the on-call, and confirms dispatch — with the full transcript to the account manager.

The agenticrx fix: Customer proof where customers decide: uptime stats, response times, and facility-manager quotes on every market page.

168 locations listed — but no Charlotte number, address, or local booking. The metro on the outreach list gets a national form.

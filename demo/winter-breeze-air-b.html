What the audit found on winterbreezeair.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

Two phone numbers

The homepage shows (954) 595-0571 while the published business number is (866) 381-3797. Which one rings the office? The customer shouldn’t have to guess — and shouldn’t have to try both.

The agenticrx fix: One primary number everywhere, tracked to the source. Every call, text, and chat lands in one inbox.
🔴 Costing you jobs

No after-hours answer

No 24/7 claim, no emergency line, and no chat were found. Fort Lauderdale heat doesn’t keep office hours — but the site assumes the customer will wait until it does.

The agenticrx fix: Nina answers in 8 seconds, captures the job, and texts a confirmation — overnight, weekends, holidays.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The quote form is 9 fields before hello

The contact form asks for name, email, phone, a dropdown, and a message — plus a captcha. On mobile, that’s a wall between a sweaty homeowner and help.

The agenticrx fix: Conversational intake that asks one thing at a time — and never shows a captcha to someone with no AC.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

15+ years, zero reviews shown

The site claims more than 15 years in the industry — yet no customer reviews, ratings, or testimonials appear. A decade and a half of goodwill is invisible at the decision moment.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage and texted review requests after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No booking, no text-back

The only digital path is the long form. No scheduling, no SMS option — the customer who won’t call and won’t fill 9 fields simply leaves.

The agenticrx fix: One-tap booking in chat plus missed-call text-back: every kind of customer gets a door.

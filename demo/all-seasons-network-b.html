What the audit found on allseasonsservicenet.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

Emergency service, office-hours chat

“Emergency Service Available” is plastered across the site — but the Podium chat routes to the office, and there’s no 24/7 claim. After hours the chat is an unmanned form and the customer must call and hope.

The agenticrx fix: Sam answers the chat 24/7, triages “emergency” vs “can wait until morning,” and books accordingly — the emergency promise finally holds at 2 AM.
🔴 Costing you jobs

No online booking

The only digital path to a job is a form. There’s no scheduling flow, no slot picker, nothing a customer can complete in 30 seconds on a phone.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat → slot offered → booked, with a confirmation text. The form stays for the detail-oriented; everyone else gets speed.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The fallback form is 20 fields long

The Gravity contact form runs to 20 fields. On a phone, at 10 PM, with a dead AC, a form that long is a bounce machine — most visitors never reach “submit.”

The agenticrx fix: Conversational intake that asks one thing at a time — name, problem, address, time — and never shows 20 boxes at once.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No reviews anywhere on the site

No ratings, testimonials, or review badges were found in the readable content. The emergency customer is asked to trust a stranger with no evidence.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage and texted review requests after every job — proof builds itself.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Two phone numbers, no guidance

Both (850) 434-0384 and 850-281-5232 appear on the site. The panicked customer has to guess which one reaches a human fastest.

The agenticrx fix: One primary tap-to-call number, tracked to the source — every call, text, and chat in a single inbox.

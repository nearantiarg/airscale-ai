What the audit found on justcoolingatl.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

“24/7 emergency service” is a phone number — and only a phone number

The site promises 24/7 emergency service with a click-to-call number. No text line, no chat, no AI answering anywhere. A homeowner with a dead AC at 1 AM either reaches a person or leaves a voicemail — and voicemails don't book jobs.

The agenticrx fix: Maya answers in 8 seconds, triages the emergency, and books the call — overnight, weekends, holidays.
🔴 Costing you jobs

Schedule and estimate requests go into a queue

The nav offers schedule service and request estimate — both submit-and-wait forms. Nothing confirms a time slot instantly, and nothing tells the customer when anyone will respond.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked, with a confirmation text before the page even closes.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No tap-to-text for mobile visitors

Over half of local searches happen on a phone, and a big share of those searchers would rather text than call. The site offers no texting option at all — call or form, take it or leave it.

The agenticrx fix: Every page gets a text-us path answered instantly by the AI, 24/7.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Your proof lives on the testimonials page, not the homepage

Testimonials exist — on a separate page most visitors never open. The homepage hero carries the 24/7 claim with no customer proof beside it.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed in the hero, auto-replies to every review, and review requests texted after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No office hours posted where the forms live

We found no posted office hours near the schedule/estimate forms — so a customer submitting at 9 PM has no idea whether anyone will read it before morning.

The agenticrx fix: The AI states response expectations upfront and acts on the request immediately, whatever the hour.

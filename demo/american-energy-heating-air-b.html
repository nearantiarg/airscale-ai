🔴 Costing you jobs

Your office goes dark at 5:30 PM

Your published hours are Monday–Friday, 8:30 AM–5:30 PM — and nothing on the site says what happens after. A Decatur homeowner with a dead AC at 8 PM gets a voicemail, and a competitor's dispatcher gets the job.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds, books the job, and texts the confirmation — nights, weekends, heat waves.
🔴 Costing you trust

Your 4.8★ reputation never appears on your own site

4.8 stars across 568 reviews sits in your business listing — but the homepage we read shows no customer quotes, no star summary, no review section. When trust is off-site, a rival's on-site reviews win the click.

The agenticrx fix: a live review feed on your homepage, auto-replies to every review, and review requests texted after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Your own stat counters read zero

The homepage's three "since 2006" counters — air conditioners repaired, furnaces repaired, systems installed — all display 0 instead of real numbers. It's the first thing a skeptical visitor notices, and it undercuts the credibility the rest of the page builds.

The agenticrx fix: live, verified numbers — jobs completed, reviews earned, average response time — pulled from your own records and kept current automatically.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No way to text or chat — call or wait

The only contact paths are a phone call or a contact form. No click-to-text, no live chat, no AI answering. More than half your visitors are on their phones at night — and they don't call; they tap.

The agenticrx fix: chat that opens in one tap, SMS fallback when the visitor won't talk, and every conversation routed to a booked slot.

A visitor with an urgent repair can't book a slot or get a price estimate — they fill out a form and wait for a callback during office hours. Every step between "I need help" and "a tech is coming" is a step where they leave.

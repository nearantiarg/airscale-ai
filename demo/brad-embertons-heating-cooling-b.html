The header promises service 7 days a week, 7 AM to 6:30 PM — and then the site goes quiet. A homeowner with a dead furnace at midnight gets no chat, no texting, no AI. Just a form that nobody reads until morning.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds, triages the no-heat call, and books the first morning slot — while the homeowner is still panicking, not after.

Every path on the site ends at a contact form

Every call-to-action — "Contact Us", "Get a Free Quote" — opens a form with no stated response time. No online booking, no live scheduling, no click-to-text. Prospects who won't fill out a form simply leave.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked. The receipt fills in live, and the owner gets a one-line summary.

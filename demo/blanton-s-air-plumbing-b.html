Texting mentioned, not one click

Text messaging is referenced on the site, but there's no click-to-text widget observed on key pages — customers have to know the number and start the thread themselves.

The agenticrx fix: One-tap texting from every page, with the AI continuing the conversation.
🔴 Costing you trust

75 years of reviews, collected manually

Reviews are displayed, but there's no automated post-job review collection visible — at 75 years, the review engine should run itself.

The agenticrx fix: Automatic review requests texted after every job, with auto-replies to each review.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No AI pre-qualification before callbacks

Estimate and service requests route to humans with no triage — staff spend the first minutes of every callback gathering basics the AI could have captured.

The agenticrx fix: The AI gathers name, issue, and urgency first — every callback starts with context.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Weekend and holiday staffing cost

24/7 coverage with humans is expensive — tier-1 calls (hours, pricing, booking) burn dispatcher time around the clock.

The agenticrx fix: The AI handles tier-1 calls and booking 24/7; humans take only the conversations that need them.

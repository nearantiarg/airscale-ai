Emergency service ends Saturday at 3 PM

Hours are Mon–Fri 7 AM–5 PM, Sat 7 AM–3 PM, Sun closed — and “emergency service” is listed as available Sat 7 AM–3 PM only. Sunday night emergencies have no path at all: no chat, no AI, no text.

The agenticrx fix: Ava triages Sunday-night emergencies, captures the job, and pages on-call — with a text confirmation.
🔴 Costing you jobs

Booking means a 7-field contact form

The contact form asks for first name, last name, phone, email, address, city, and comments — a homework assignment before a conversation. No scheduler, no chat shortcut.

The agenticrx fix: Chat asks three questions — what’s wrong, where, when — and books. The receipt fills in live.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

A product catalog, not a booking experience

The nav leads with Products, Product Knowledge, Thermostats & Zoning — shopping aisles for a customer who arrived with a broken system and wants a booked visit.

The agenticrx fix: One action leads: book the visit in 30 seconds — with product detail one tap away, not in the way.

Reviews exist — as scattered quotes

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage, auto-replies to every review, and review requests texted after every job.

What the audit found on rescueheatingandair.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

The 24/7 line is call-only

The site advertises “Call Us Now 24/7” — but there’s no chat, no AI, no text path. A customer who won’t (or can’t) call at 2 AM gets the “Send Us A Message” form instead.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds by voice or chat, captures the job, and texts a confirmation — overnight, weekends, holidays.
🔴 Costing you jobs

No online booking anywhere

Service pages for AC, heating, water heaters, generators, and marine HVAC — and zero way to book any of them online. Every job starts with a phone call or a form submission.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked. The receipt fills in live, and the owner gets a one-line summary.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Four phone numbers, no single action

The header lists a toll-free line plus separate MS and LA numbers — (228) 678-3278, (225) 665-2373, (985) 231-2206. A visitor has to decide which to call instead of tapping one booking button.

The agenticrx fix: One chat entry point that routes by location automatically — the customer never picks a number.
🔴 Costing you trust

No reviews on the homepage at all

We found no testimonials, no Google badge, no rating anywhere on the homepage. For a company selling trust across two states, that’s a silent page.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage, auto-replies to every review, and review requests texted after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The message form is a black box

“Send Us A Message” gives no response-time promise — no “we reply within X” anywhere. After-hours senders have no reason to believe anyone’s listening.

The agenticrx fix: Every chat gets an instant, specific reply — and the owner sees a one-line summary the moment it lands.

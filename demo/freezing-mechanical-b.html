What the audit found on freezingmechanical.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you trust

Your review count argues with itself

The homepage claims "over 1500 5-star reviews" in one section and "600+ Five-Star Reviews" in another. A shopper who notices both numbers stops believing either — and your best trust asset goes to waste.

The agenticrx fix: one verified number, pulled live from your review profile and shown in a badge that updates itself.
🔴 Costing you jobs

A typo on the page that asks for the call

The service-area page reads "please call us at please call us at 305-363-7021" — and the footer adds a third number, (305) 563-7843. The panicked caller with a dead AC is staring at three different numbers, unsure which one works.

The agenticrx fix: one number, one tap-to-call button, one text line — and Ava answers whichever one they choose.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

"Schedule online" means fill out a form

Book-an-estimate and contact forms are the whole online path — no live slots, no instant confirmation, no text-back. The midnight caller submits a form into the dark and waits until morning.

The agenticrx fix: real slots in chat, booked in under a minute, with a text confirmation before they close the tab.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The maintenance plan has no price

The plan page sells priority service, up-to-20% discounts, and 2–3 annual visits — but no plan cost. The value pitch ends at "call us," and most shoppers never make the call.

The agenticrx fix: plan tiers with real prices in chat — the signup closes where the pitch happens.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No SMS path on a 24/7 site

Hours are listed as Mo–Su 00:00–24:00, yet contact is call-or-form only — no text line. The homeowner who won't pick up the phone at 1 AM has no way to reach you at all.

The agenticrx fix: a text line answered by Ava 24/7 — every after-hours message becomes a booked job, not a missed one.

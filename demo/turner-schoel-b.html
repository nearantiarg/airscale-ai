The agenticrx fix: Ada answers in 8 seconds, captures the emergency with address and details, and texts a confirmation — the 24/7 promise finally holds.

Schema claims 5 stars — the homepage shows none

The page markup claims a 5/5 rating, yet no reviews, quotes, or badges appear in the readable page. Google may show the stars; the customer on your site sees none of them.

The agenticrx fix: Real review quotes pulled onto the homepage automatically — the markup claim becomes visible proof.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Deals are advertised — claiming one requires a call

The site advertises deals on AC repair, AC installation, and service — but there’s no online path to ask about or claim one. Every deal-seeker must dial first.

The agenticrx fix: Chat-based deal capture: “which deal caught your eye?” → details → booked estimate. The promotion finally converts.

No text-back for the customer who won’t call

No SMS path was found. A large share of homeowners — especially younger ones — would rather text than call; they currently bounce.

The agenticrx fix: Missed-call text-back plus on-site text chat: every “I’d rather text” visitor becomes a lead.

One number for three towns, no triage

Northport to Samantha funnel through a single line with no way to signal location or urgency up front. The dispatcher learns the address only after answering.

The agenticrx fix: Intake captures town, problem, and urgency before anyone picks up — dispatch starts with context.

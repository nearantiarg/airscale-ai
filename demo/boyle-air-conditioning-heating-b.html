The agenticrx fix: Ruby answers in 8 seconds, triages the urgency, and books the job — the 24/7 promise finally has someone behind it.
🔴 Costing you jobs

Messenger chat sleeps when you do

The Facebook Messenger widget only helps while someone is watching it. A message sent at midnight sits unread until morning — by which time the customer has called someone else.

The agenticrx fix: AI answers every chat instantly, 24/7 — and hands the full transcript to the office in the morning.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The contact form asks for a phone number — not what’s broken

The form collects name and phone and nothing else: no service type, no problem description, no address. The tech calls back blind, and the customer waits for a second conversation to explain the first.

The agenticrx fix: Chat-based intake: what’s wrong, where, and when — so the tech arrives with context and the job is half-sold.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

BBB A+ is claimed, but no customer voices are shown

The A+ rating is mentioned in the site text, yet the homepage shows no reviews or testimonials to back it up at the decision moment. Proof that lives off-site might as well not exist.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage, auto-replies to every review, and review requests texted after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Every path funnels to the same phone line

No online booking or scheduling was found. Customers who’d rather tap than talk — a big share of homeowners under 40 — bounce to a competitor with a “book now” button.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat → slot offered → booked, with a confirmation text. The phone stays for the callers; everyone else gets a door too.

What the audit found on busbys.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

After Saturday at 5 PM, it’s one phone line

Published hours are Mon–Fri 8–7, Sat 8–5 — then emergency service runs 24/7 by phone only. No chat, no text-back, no booking. If the line is busy at 11 PM on a Sunday, the job goes to whoever picks up.

The agenticrx fix: Hank answers in 8 seconds, captures the emergency, and texts a confirmation — the 24/7 promise finally has coverage behind it.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

80 years of reputation, zero reviews on the site

The site leans on “over 80 years” and “#1” — but no customer reviews, ratings, or testimonials appear in the readable homepage. Eight decades of goodwill are invisible at the decision moment.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage and texted review requests after every job — eighty years of proof, finally visible.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Scheduling is mentioned — a schedule-able path isn’t

Online scheduling language appears on the site, yet no bookable calendar or scheduling widget was detectable on the homepage. The customer reads “schedule” and still has to call.

The agenticrx fix: Real slot offers in chat — “Tuesday between 9 and 11?” — booked in 30 seconds with a confirmation text.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No text-back for the call-shy customer

No SMS or text path was found. Homeowners who’d rather type than talk — especially after hours — have no door at all.

The agenticrx fix: Missed-call text-back plus on-site text chat: the “I’d rather text” customer becomes a captured lead instead of a bounce.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The emergency caller gets no triage

At 2 AM there’s no way to signal “no heat with kids in the house” versus “routine tune-up question” before someone picks up. Every call gets the same queue — urgent or not.

The agenticrx fix: AI triage captures urgency, address, and system details up front — the on-call tech sees the real emergencies first.

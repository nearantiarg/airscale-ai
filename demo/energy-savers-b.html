What the audit found on energysaversair.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

No one greets the visitor

No chat widget or AI was found anywhere on the site. The emergency customer’s only move is to fill out a form and wait — at the exact moment speed matters most.

The agenticrx fix: June greets every visitor instantly, answers the “is this an emergency?” question, and captures the job in under a minute.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The emergency promise runs on a form

The site says “HVAC breakdowns happen at inconvenient times” — but the inconvenient-time customer gets a form, not a conversation. A form submitted at midnight gets read in the morning.

The agenticrx fix: Chat-based emergency intake with instant confirmation text — the customer knows help is coming before they close the tab.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The 5-star rating has no voice

The page markup claims a 5/5 rating and a Google review is mentioned — yet no actual customer quotes appear on the homepage. A number without a voice doesn’t persuade.

The agenticrx fix: Real review quotes pulled onto the homepage automatically, plus texted review requests after every job to keep them coming.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Same-day service is advertised — availability isn’t shown

The meta description promises same-day service across West Georgia and East Alabama, but nothing on the page shows live availability. The customer can’t tell whether “same-day” means today.

The agenticrx fix: Live slot offers in chat — “we can be there between 2 and 4 today” — so the promise becomes a booking.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No text-back option

No SMS or text path was found. A large share of homeowners would rather text than call or fill a form — they currently have no door at all.

The agenticrx fix: Missed-call text-back plus on-site text chat: every “I’d rather text” customer becomes a captured lead.

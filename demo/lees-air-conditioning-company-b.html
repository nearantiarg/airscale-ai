What the audit found on leesac.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

After hours = an answering service

Office hours are Mon–Fri, 8 AM–5 PM. After that, an answering service takes a message — a voicemail, not a booking. The urgent caller waits.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds, captures the job, and texts a confirmation — the message becomes a booked visit.
🔴 Costing you jobs

“Free estimate” — with no way to claim it

Free estimates on replacement are advertised, but there’s no online booking, no quote form, no chat. Every estimate starts with a phone call.

The agenticrx fix: Chat-based estimate capture — address, system age, preferred time — so the tech arrives with context and the job is half-sold.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The warranty excludes the hours you need it most

The extended labor warranty’s fine print excludes “after hours service” — exactly when most breakdowns happen.

The agenticrx fix: Not a website fix — but the site should say it plainly instead of burying it two scrolls down.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Three anonymous quotes are the whole proof

Customer reviews are three first-initial quotes with no ratings, no dates, no source. Thin evidence for a company serving Louisiana for decades.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage, auto-replies to every review, and review requests texted after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The call-to-action drowns in SEO copy

Long keyword-heavy sections push the “call Lee’s AC today” ask further down with every scroll. Visitors bounce before they reach it.

The agenticrx fix: A rebuilt page that leads with one action — book in 30 seconds — with the SEO kept where Google needs it, not where humans trip on it.

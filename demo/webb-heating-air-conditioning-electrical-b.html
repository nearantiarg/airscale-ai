Three different phone numbers on the site

The site shows (336) 439-6150, (336) 390-3875, and (336) 390-3917 across pages. Three numbers means missed calls, confused customers, and broken tracking.

The agenticrx fix: One number everywhere, answered by AI around the clock — so 'which number' stops mattering.
🔴 Costing you trust

The site warns visitors to enable JavaScript

The page carries a 'please enable JavaScript for a better experience' notice. For visitors on locked-down work phones or slow connections, that's a friction warning before the pitch.

The agenticrx fix: A rebuilt page that works with or without JavaScript — the booking path never depends on the visitor's settings.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

'Schedule Online' exists — but there's no chat for questions first

Online scheduling is in place, which beats most competitors. But the shopper with one question — 'do you service my area?' — still has to call.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers the pre-booking questions in chat and hands off a warm, qualified booking.

Reviews live on a separate page, not the homepage

The site has a reviews page, but the homepage shows no rating, no count, no quotes. The proof exists — visitors just never see it.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed in the hero with the count and platform named — verified, not claimed.

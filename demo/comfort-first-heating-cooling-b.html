What the audit found on yourcomfortfirst.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

No live answering after hours

No live chat or instant answering was detected. "Schedule Now" and the estimate form are the only paths — after hours, both are messages in bottles.

The agenticrx fix: Ollie answers in 8 seconds, books the slot, and texts a confirmation — overnight, weekends, holidays.
🔴 Costing you jobs

A nine-field form stands between you and a tech

The free-estimate form asks for nine fields — first name, last name, email, phone, full address, zip, location, message — before the customer gets anything. That's a stall, not a start.

The agenticrx fix: Chat asks three things — what's wrong, where, when — and books the visit. The rest comes later.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Pick your region before you get help

Five service regions — NC Triangle, Triad, Charlotte, Jacksonville, Outer Banks. The visitor must pick a location before seeing anything local. Geography first, help second.

The agenticrx fix: The AI detects the service area from the address and routes automatically — no region picker.
🟡 Costing you trust

Ninety years of trust, none at the booking

50+ years — in business since 1931 — is the trust story. But the booking flow shows no reviews at the decision point. Nine decades of reputation, zero of it where it counts.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed beside every booking button, plus review requests texted after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Financing never enters the estimate form

Financing exists — but it's a separate page from the estimate request. The customer doing the scariest math gets no payment path inside the form.

The agenticrx fix: The AI quotes the monthly payment the moment the estimate lands.

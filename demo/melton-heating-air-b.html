What the audit found on meltonheatingac.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

No office hours anywhere on the site

The homepage lists a phone number and an email — but no hours, no days, nothing telling a visitor whether you're open right now. A Kennesaw homeowner at 9 PM has to guess whether calling is even worth it.

The agenticrx fix: a live "open now / booking now" status on every page — plus Ava answering 24/7 when the office is closed.
🔴 Costing you jobs

No way to book online

The only paths are calling or emailing — no request form, no booking calendar, no chat, no AI answering. Every visitor who won't pick up the phone is a visitor who leaves.

The agenticrx fix: one tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked. The receipt fills in live, and the owner gets a one-line summary.
🔴 Costing you trust

"5 Star Ratings" — with no proof attached

The homepage claims 5-star ratings but shows no platform, no review count, and no customer quotes. A trust claim a visitor can't verify reads like a placeholder — and your real reviews deserve better.

The agenticrx fix: a live review feed on your homepage, auto-replies to every review, and review requests texted after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Trust badges your visitors can't read

The Georgia Power Partner, insurance, and financing badges are images with no readable text — a phone screen shows them as decoration, not credentials. The credibility is real; the site doesn't let anyone see it.

The agenticrx fix: credentials as real text with links — readable on any phone, indexed by search engines, and clickable into proof.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No service-area list

Nothing on the page says which towns you cover beyond Kennesaw. A homeowner two zip codes away can't tell whether to call — and the search engines that send local traffic can't tell either.

The agenticrx fix: a real service-area map and town list — with Ava confirming coverage instantly when a visitor types their address.

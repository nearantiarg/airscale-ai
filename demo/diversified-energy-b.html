🔴 Costing you jobs

No online booking observed

Audits, insulation quotes, Manual J — everything routes through contact forms and phone. High-consideration services need low-friction starts.

The agenticrx fix: “Book my energy audit” in chat: address → slot → confirmed, in under a minute.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

“We're hiring!” competes with “call us”

A hiring banner shares the header with the customer phone number. Job seekers and paying customers get equal billing — they shouldn't.

The agenticrx fix: Separate the flows: customers get instant booking; applicants get an application path. Both handled by AI.

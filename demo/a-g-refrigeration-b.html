What the audit found on agrefrigeration.net — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🟡 Leaving money on the table

“Book Service” is a callback request

The booking button opens a form asking “When should we callback?” — morning, noon, or afternoon. It’s a request for a phone call, not a booking.

The agenticrx fix: Real booking: pick a slot in chat, get a confirmation text. No callback limbo.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Evening hours are members-only

Open 7 AM–5 PM; extended hours run only for service-agreement customers. Everyone else is phone-only after 5.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers every caller — member or not — and books the visit on the spot, any hour.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The form asks for everything, promises nothing

Name, phone, address, service needed — and no response-time commitment in return. High effort, low trust.

The agenticrx fix: Chat asks three questions, offers a slot, and confirms — the effort-to-answer ratio flips.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

“No. 1 since 1968” — with no proof on the page

Big heritage claims, but no reviews, no ratings, no testimonials anywhere on the homepage.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage, auto-replies to every review, and review requests texted after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Self-serve for bills, not for booking

There’s a “Pay my bill” path for existing customers — but a new customer can’t book without waiting for a callback.

The agenticrx fix: The same one-tap convenience for new jobs: book in chat, pay online, done.

What the audit found on priorityheatandair.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you jobs

24/7 is a club perk, not a promise

‘Priority service 24/7, 365 days a year’ belongs to the $200/year Service Club. Non-members get the quote form: ‘we'll get back to you as soon as possible.’ The emergency caller isn't a member yet.

The agenticrx fix: Ava qualifies urgency for everyone, 24/7 — members get priority dispatch, everyone else gets booked. Nobody waits on a form.
🔴 Costing you jobs

The quote form has no response-time promise

No booking, no chat, no text — the form is the funnel, and it doesn't say when anyone replies. ‘As soon as possible’ is not a commitment.

The agenticrx fix: One tap in chat → slot offered → name → booked, with a text confirmation the second it lands.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

One testimonial on the whole homepage

Meg C.'s quote is lovely — but it's the only one, next to a ‘Five Star Google Reviews’ image with no rating shown. Forty years of Kendall Smith's reputation, compressed to one quote.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage, auto-replies to every review, and review requests texted after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

$200/year plan, no online signup

The maintenance agreement is priced on the page — but joining requires a call or the form. The easiest yes on the site has the hardest path.

The agenticrx fix: Chat-based plan signup — address, system age, done — with the first tune-up booked on the spot.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No hours published

Nothing says when the office is staffed. Visitors can't tell a staffed line from an on-call gamble — so they assume the worst and keep scrolling.

The agenticrx fix: A page that states hours clearly — with Ava covering every hour the office doesn't.

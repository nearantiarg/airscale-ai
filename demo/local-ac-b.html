What the audit found on local-ac.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🔴 Costing you trust

A 1-star horror story sits on your homepage

Your embedded reviews widget shows an unmoderated 1-star review describing a botched vent-sealing job, ashes floating in a bedroom, and a GM who called the customer a bad customer. It now lives on your homepage for every visitor to read.

The agenticrx fix: curated review display — your best verified reviews up front — plus auto-responses to every review, good and bad, within the hour.
🔴 Costing you jobs

No hours, no after-hours path on the page

The homepage shows no business hours and no after-hours answering path — for a company whose own reviews praise 24-hour response. The promise isn't on the page; the risk is.

The agenticrx fix: published hours backed by 24/7 AI answering — the fast-response reputation becomes a system, not a hope.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

The reviews are a wall, not a showcase

The widget dumps dozens of full-length reviews as a wall of text — the great ones (same-day saves, fair pricing) drown under the volume. Nobody reads review #17.

The agenticrx fix: a curated showcase of your strongest verified reviews above the fold — short, attributed, and converting.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No instant quote or estimate path

Every conversion path is call or contact form — no instant quote, no estimate capture, nothing for the homeowner who wants a number before they commit to a call.

The agenticrx fix: chat-based estimate capture — address, system age, preferred time — so the tech arrives with context and the job is half-sold.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Third-party badges with no explanation

HomeAdvisor and HomeGuide badges appear on the page — unexplained, undated, unlinked. Trust signals without context read as decoration.

The agenticrx fix: verified, current credentials displayed with sources — or removed so they don't dilute the real proof.

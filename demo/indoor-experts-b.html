Your online booking calendar doesn't work

Your own site says it outright: "The online booking calendar needs JavaScript. Call (404) 913-5802 to book your visit by phone." A visitor who came to book online gets told to pick up the phone instead — and many of them just leave.

The agenticrx fix: booking that actually works in the chat — one tap, a slot, a name, done. No scripts required on the visitor's end.
🔴 Costing you jobs

No overnight line — your own FAQ admits it

Your FAQ says "there's no overnight line," and hours are Mon–Fri 8–6, Sat 10–4. An Atlanta AC dies at 1 AM in August: your 4.8★ reputation is asleep, and the company that answers gets the emergency job.

The agenticrx fix: Ava answers in 8 seconds, books the job, and texts the confirmation — nights, weekends, August.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Phone is the only after-hours path

No chat, no click-to-text, no AI answering. A homeowner with a failing furnace at 9 PM can call and hope — or text a competitor's AI and get a booked slot in 30 seconds. Phone-only loses the customers who won't call.

The agenticrx fix: chat that opens in one tap, SMS fallback for the caller who won't talk, and every conversation routed to a booked slot.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

"Get My Quote" is a form, not a quote

Your quote button sends the visitor to a contact form — no estimate, no price range, no callback window. In a competitive Atlanta market, the shop that gives a number first usually gets the job.

The agenticrx fix: an instant estimate in chat, trained on your real pricing bands — then a one-tap booking before the visitor cools off.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Emergency service with no fast lane

You list "Fast emergency AC repair" as a service — but there's no separate emergency number, no priority path, no "call now" distinction from routine bookings. An emergency customer needs to feel they're being routed somewhere faster.

The agenticrx fix: urgency-aware routing — dead ACs and gas smells get their own fast path to a booked tech, day or night.

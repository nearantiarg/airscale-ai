🔴 Costing you jobs

Podium chat, office-hours only

The Podium chat widget routes to the office — and there’s no 24/7 claim. After hours the chat is an unmanned form, and the 21-field Gravity form is the only door left.

The agenticrx fix: Moe answers the chat 24/7 — capturing the emergency, the address, and the booking while the office sleeps.
🔴 Costing you jobs

The form is 21 fields

Twenty-one fields — one of the longest in this entire audit. On a phone, on a Sunday, with no AC, that form is a guaranteed bounce: most visitors never reach “submit.”

The agenticrx fix: Conversational intake that asks one thing at a time — done in under a minute, on any phone.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Two phone numbers

Both (803) 753-4830 and (843) 755-6879 appear. The Sumter customer and the Florence customer each have to guess which number is theirs.

The agenticrx fix: One primary tap-to-call number, tracked to the source — every call, text, and chat in a single inbox.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Emergency AC repair is promised — triage isn’t

“Emergency air conditioner repair” is advertised, but there’s no way to signal “elderly parent, no AC, 95°” before someone calls back. Every emergency waits in the same queue.

The agenticrx fix: AI triage captures urgency, household situation, and address up front — the truly urgent calls surface first.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

No reviews on the site

No ratings, testimonials, or review badges were found in the readable content. The emergency customer is asked to trust a stranger — with no evidence — at the worst moment.

The agenticrx fix: A live review feed on the homepage and texted review requests after every job.

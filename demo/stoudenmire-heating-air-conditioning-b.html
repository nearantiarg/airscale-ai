What the audit found on stoudenmireheating.com — and what the agenticrx version does about each one.

🟡 Leaving money on the table

Every promo is phone-gated

$25 off repairs, the $50 referral bonus, the $170 maintenance plan — every one says “Must mention this ad. Call for full details & exclusions.” The discounts that should convert online visitors only convert callers.

The agenticrx fix: promo codes that apply themselves in chat — the discount closes the booking instead of gating it.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

“Fill out our form for us to contact you”

The request path is “simply fill out our request form for us to contact you” — no calendar, no time slots, no confirmation. The visitor submits and waits; urgency leaks out.

The agenticrx fix: chat-based booking — slot offered, name captured, confirmation texted. No waiting room.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

Testimonials without proof markers

Customer testimonials carry names but no star ratings, no platforms, no dates — warm words that can't be verified. Anonymous-format praise is the weakest social proof.

The agenticrx fix: verified reviews with names, dates, and platform — displayed live on the homepage and growing after every job.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

After 9 PM, the 24/7 promise is one phone

Office hours run to 9:00 PM — after that, 24/7 emergency is a single phone line with no text or chat fallback. If the line's busy, the job goes elsewhere.

The agenticrx fix: an AI that answers every after-hours contact, triages the emergency, and books the visit — so 24/7 is real.
🟡 Leaving money on the table

“FREE Online System Quote” isn't instant

The free online system quote is a form submission — the highest-intent action on the site ends in a wait. A homeowner comparing systems wants a number, not a callback queue.

The agenticrx fix: chat-based estimate capture — home details, system age, timeline — so the quote conversation starts in seconds.
